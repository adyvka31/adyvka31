### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Software Engineer & Systems Architect** based in Indonesia, specializing in designing and implementing scalable, high-concurrency web and cross-platform applications. My engineering philosophy revolves around **Clean Architecture**, **SOLID principles**, and maintainable domain-driven design.

Currently completing my vocational degree in Software Engineering at **SMK IDN Boarding School** (maintaining an academic honors record of **96/100 GPA**), I bridge academic rigor with real-world enterprise engineering — from architecting mission-critical ERP systems to authoring technical books and mentoring the next generation of engineers.

- 💼 **Professional Engagements:**
  - **Full Stack Developer** at **PT Ardyanda Digital Inovasi** (Engineering internal financial ledgers, payroll, and healthcare platforms).
  - **Full Stack Engineer** at **PT Intisel Prodaktifakom** (Architected the *Databank ERP* system; NestJS, PostgreSQL, Docker, and Huawei ISDP integration).
- 🏆 **National Honors:**
  - 🥇 **1st Place Champion** — National Web Design Competition (*ICOMFEST 2025*).
  - 🥈 **2nd Place Runner-Up** — National Web Design Competition (*PNBITC#18 2026*, Politeknik Negeri Bali).
  - 🏅 **Top 5 National Finalist** — National Web Dev Competition (*Universitas Lampung 2024*).
- 📚 **Author & Educator:** Published 2 technical books (*MIT Scratch* & *Framework-based Web Development*) and mentored 100+ students across schools in Jakarta & West Java.
- 🎓 **Credentials:** **Harvard University CS50** Computer Science Graduate & **AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner Essentials**.
- 🔭 **Current Focus:** Distributed Systems, Microservices, Cloud Security (OWASP Top 10), and GenAI/LLM-integrated workflows.
